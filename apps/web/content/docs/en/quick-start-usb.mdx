---
title: "Quick start: USB-RS485"
description: Read the first register in 5 minutes, nothing to install.
---

You need: desktop Chrome, Edge or Firefox 151+; a USB-RS485 converter (FTDI preferred); the slave's ID, baud rate and parity (from the manual or the device's display).

<Steps>
<li>
Plug the converter into USB and wire A and B to the device.
<Result>Windows installs the FTDI driver by itself; on Linux the port shows up as /dev/ttyUSB0.</Result>
</li>
<li>
Open ModField and, on the first screen, choose **USB-RS485 on this computer** (key <Kbd>1</Kbd>).
<Result>The Connection module opens on the RTU tab.</Result>
</li>
<li>
Set **Baud rate**, **Parity** and **Stop bits** to match the device.
<Result>The values are saved in the profile.</Result>
</li>
<li>
Click **Choose port** and pick the converter in the browser's list.
<Result>The manufacturer and VID:PID appear next to it, e.g. "FTDI 0403:6001".</Result>
</li>
<li>
Click **Connect** (<Kbd>Ctrl+Enter</Kbd>).
<Result>The status bar shows "● CONNECTED".</Result>
</li>
<li>
Open **Read** (<Kbd>2</Kbd>), **Block** tab: enter Unit ID, Table and Address and press <Kbd>R</Kbd>.
<Result>The table shows the raw and the decoded value. If TIMEOUT shows up, see [troubleshooting](/en/docs/troubleshooting#timeout).</Result>
</li>
</Steps>
