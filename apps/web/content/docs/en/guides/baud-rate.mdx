---
title: Find the baud rate and parity
description: Try the common combinations, or just listen to a master that is already there.
---

## Trying (bus without another master)

<Steps>
<li>
Choose the port in Connection (no need to connect).
<Result>The port shows with manufacturer and VID:PID.</Result>
</li>
<li>
In **Scanner** › **Serial settings**, enter the likely IDs (1, 2, 247 by default) and click **Start scan**.
<Result>ModField reopens the port with each combination, from the common ones (9600 8N1, 19200 8E1…) to the rare ones.</Result>
</li>
<li>
When "Found: …" appears, click **Apply to profile and connect**.
<Result>The profile gets the right settings and the connection opens.</Result>
</li>
</Steps>

## Just listening (a PLC or SCADA is already on the bus)

In **Passive listening**, ModField transmits nothing: it looks for CRC-valid frames under each combination. Use it when you must not disturb the master that is already there.
