---
title: Log for 24 hours
description: A long log that keeps going with the tab closed.
---

<Callout type="warning">For more than 1 hour, use the agent. Without it, the browser slows down timers in background tabs and the log ends up with gaps.</Callout>

<Steps>
<li>
Connect through the agent (RTU through the agent, or TCP).
<Result>The Logger shows "Collected by the agent".</Result>
</li>
<li>
Disable notebook sleep.
<Result>Asleep, nothing is collected and the interval shows as a gap.</Result>
</li>
<li>
In **Logger** (<Kbd>7</Kbd>), give a **Name**, choose **Interval** (1000 ms), **Duration** (1440 min) and the tags.
<Result>A week at 1 s with 50 tags takes less than 200 MB on the agent.</Result>
</li>
<li>
Click **Start logging** (<Kbd>L</Kbd>).
<Result>The status bar shows "log ● name · samples · agent".</Result>
</li>
<li>
Mark events with <Kbd>M</Kbd>: "valve 50% open".
<Result>The marker shows in the chart and goes into the report.</Result>
</li>
<li>
You may close the tab. When you come back, ModField fetches everything the agent collected.
<Result>The sample counter jumps to the total; nothing is lost.</Result>
</li>
</Steps>

<Shot src="/docs/en/logger.png" alt="Logger with trend chart, marker and window statistics." />

In the chart, drag to zoom, use ← → to pan and **Show all** to go back. **Export CSV** writes ISO 8601 timestamp, tag, value and quality.
