---
title: Find an unknown slave
description: Scan IDs 1 to 247 and see who answers.
---

<Steps>
<li>
Connect with the serial settings you believe are right.
<Result>"CONNECTED" in the status bar.</Result>
</li>
<li>
Open **Scanner** (<Kbd>4</Kbd>), **IDs** tab.
<Result>From 1 to 247, short timeout by baud rate (60 ms at 19200).</Result>
</li>
<li>
Click **Start scan** and confirm the warning.
<Result>IDs 1, 2 and 247 go first; the ones found appear in the table during the scan.</Result>
</li>
<li>
Click **Add to equipment** on each ID found.
<Result>In Connection › Equipment, use **Read identification** (function 43/14) to fill in manufacturer, model and firmware.</Result>
</li>
</Steps>

A slave that "answered with exception 02" exists: it just has no holding register 0. If no ID answers, the serial settings are probably wrong: [find the baud rate](/en/docs/guides/baud-rate).
