---
title: Test without equipment
description: Slave simulator for a SCADA, a PLC or ModField itself.
---

Without the agent, use **Just try it (demo mode)**: a drive, a pressure transmitter and a flow meter simulated in the browser.

With the agent, the **Simulator** module (<Kbd>5</Kbd>) creates real slaves:

<Steps>
<li>
Click **New from the demo bench**.
<Result>The register map appears, with one generator per register.</Result>
</li>
<li>
Edit slaves and registers: table, address, type, order and generator (fixed, ramp, sine, random, counter).
<Result>Changes are saved and go into the project file.</Result>
</li>
<li>
Choose TCP (127.0.0.1:5020) or RTU on a serial port and click **Start simulator**.
<Result>It shows under "Running simulators"; the requests received are listed.</Result>
</li>
<li>
Point the SCADA at 127.0.0.1:5020, or click **Connect ModField to this simulator**.
<Result>Reads and writes show in the simulator's log.</Result>
</li>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning">Exposing the simulator on the local network makes the Windows firewall ask for administrator rights. By default it only listens on 127.0.0.1.</Callout>
