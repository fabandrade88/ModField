---
title: Make the commissioning report
description: A PDF ready to print and sign, in a few minutes.
---

<Steps>
<li>
Once only: in **Settings** › **Company**, fill in the data and upload the logo (PNG or SVG).
<Result>They appear in the header of every report.</Result>
</li>
<li>
In **Report** (<Kbd>8</Kbd>), click **New report** and fill in client, plant, area and work order.
<Result>The number follows the MF-year-sequence pattern.</Result>
</li>
<li>
Choose the test's **Log session**.
<Result>Trends, statistics, markers and the writes of that period go into the PDF.</Result>
</li>
<li>
Add the **acceptance tests**: tag, expected, tolerance (absolute or %) and measured (**Read now**, or **Mean**/**Min**/**Max** of a stretch between markers).
<Result>The result is computed. A manual verdict needs a justification, which is printed.</Result>
</li>
<li>
In **Signatures**, leave blank to sign on paper, or use **Sign on screen**.
<Result>A drawn signature is a picture, not a certified digital signature.</Result>
</li>
<li>
Click **Refresh preview**, check it and **Generate PDF** (<Kbd>G</Kbd>).
<Result>The PDF carries page X of Y, the SHA-256 hash of the data and, with the agent paired, its signature.</Result>
</li>
</Steps>

**Save as this client's template** keeps sections, tests and signatures for the next job.
