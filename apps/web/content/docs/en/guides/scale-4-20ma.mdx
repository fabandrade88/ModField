---
title: Scale a 4–20 mA signal
description: The transmitter's raw value in engineering units.
---

Many devices deliver the loop in counts (e.g. 4000 to 20000 µA) rather than in the process unit.

<Steps>
<li>
Select the tag in the **Tags** tab of Read.
<Result>The properties panel shows type, order, unit and scale.</Result>
</li>
<li>
Tick **Linear scale** and fill in **Raw min** 4000, **Raw max** 20000, **Eng. min** 0 and **Eng. max** with the full scale (e.g. 10).
<Result>The value now shows in engineering units.</Result>
</li>
<li>
Fill in **Unit** (bar) and **Decimals** (2).
<Result>The table shows "6.25 bar".</Result>
</li>
</Steps>

The scale applies to writes too: writing 50 Hz to a tag scaled 0–6000 → 0–60 Hz sends 5000.
